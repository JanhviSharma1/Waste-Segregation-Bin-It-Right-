Know Your Bins

Understanding waste segregation is the first step towards a cleaner planet!
Press "Enter" to see the next bin.

Green Bin

Use the Green Bin for wet and biodegradable waste. This bin helps in composting and recycling organic materials.

  • Banana Peels
  • Vegetable Scraps
  • Tea Bags
  • Egg Shells

Blue Bin

Dispose of dry and recyclable materials in the Blue Bin. These items can be processed and reused to reduce waste.

  • Plastic Bottles
  • Cardboard
  • Newspapers
  • Tin Cans

Red Bin

Hazardous waste goes into the Red Bin. Proper disposal prevents harm to the environment and human health.

  • Batteries
  • Broken Glass
  • Paint Cans
  • Expired Medicines

Black Bin

Use the Black Bin for non-recyclable and landfill waste. These items cannot be composted or recycled.

  • Candy Wrappers
  • Styrofoam
  • Used Diapers
  • Old Toothbrushes
